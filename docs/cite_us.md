# Cite us

ToulligQC is developed by the
[GenomiqueENS core facility](https://genomique.biologie.ens.fr/) of the
[Institute of Biology of the École Normale Supérieure (IBENS)](http://www.ibens.bio.ens.psl.eu/).

If you use ToulligQC in your work, please cite the project and its authors:

Karine Dias, Bérengère Laffay, Lionel Ferrato-Berberian, Sophie Lemoine,
Ali Hamraoui, Morgane Thomas-Chollier, Stéphane Le Crom and Laurent Jourdren.

## How to cite

Until a dedicated publication is available, please cite the software repository:






> ToulligQC — quality control for Oxford Nanopore sequencing.
> GenomiqueENS, IBENS. <https://github.com/GenomiqueENS/toulligQC>
